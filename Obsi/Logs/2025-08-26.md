#### Player Interaction Script
- It controls the ray of the camera and interacts with objects.
- Depending on ray it enables and disables the outline of the objects.
- Player throws the item in hand with the TryThrow() method in this script. 
#### UI Manager (Singleton)
- Learned about Singletons[^3]
- UI Manager regions --> Panel Management and Mini game Management
- Shake UI Panel Method gives a cool but unprofessional UI shake to desired panels
#### Interact System (IInteractable)
- Learned about Interfaces[^2]
- Added this interfaces to interactable object's classes such as vehicle, item, trash bin, boxes.
#### Vehicle System
- This system is about providing player to travel between scenes 
- A map opens and player decide a stage to play. Once the player makes a tour between Junkyard/base to stage a day starts and on return to base day ends. (Not implemented yet)
- Player also deposits the trash from the bin to the vehicle.
#### Audio Manager (Singleton)
- In this script you can store 2d or 3d sounds. With a few exceptions audio clips are stored here.
- Created a system called "Item Sound" and you put "MadeOf" sounds here. Basically choose what type of sound u need in that specific item in its Scriptable-Object.
- You also can add multiple sound effects for any type of material of the item which makes it realistic and boosts the quality of the sounds. (Type Can--> Can1.mp3 Can2.mp3 Can3.mp3)
#### Item/Trash System
- It includes TrashData and PickableItem (IInteractable)
- TrashData has many properties such as *value*, *recyclability*, *toxicity, rarity, weight, material that is made of (such as glass/metal/plastic/etc).* 
- PickableItem hold the data of the trash and manages its properties (When player is holding it change its position/transform etc.)
#### Mini Game System
- U can add this system to boxes and upon completing to mini game it gives u reward based on boxdata rng (each box type has different box data/rng)
#### Trash Bin System
-  Lid open and close animation has been made with animator. Enhanced with camera shake and sound effect,
- Trash can be deposited to vehicle when beside of the vehicle.
